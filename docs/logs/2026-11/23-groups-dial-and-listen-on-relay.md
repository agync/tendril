groups: dial and listen on relay sibling links:

- **Dial (`crates/tendril-runtime/src/group.rs`):** a `/v2/relay` manifest hint is dialed with a per-sibling `RelayV2Dialer` on the target's `group_mailbox` at the current `seq` and the pool's `relay_auth`; without the current key such hints are dropped. Any other relay hint is now dialed with the target's manifest-pinned `relay_pub` appended (manifests carry no `#` fragment, so `/v1/relay` sibling dials never worked before). An admin push over an ordinary Wire v2 session dials a `/v2/relay` hint on the ordinary roster entry's own mailbox.
- **Listen:** `GroupPoolConfig::with_relay_listen(true)` registers the group mailbox on each `/v2/relay` host of the local member's own entry (while it is an active, CRL-clean member holding the current key) and the identity's relay mailbox on each other relay host, one per canonical relay host. Group mailboxes are registered only in groups whose `min_wire_version` is 2, the floor at which siblings dial them. The book watcher reconciles registrations on every tick (rotation, removal, expiry of the manifest for both kinds), releases every registration while a refresh fails closed (for example an unresolvable root CRL), respawns a finished listener no sooner than 5 s later, and close aborts them; a listener task starts only while the pool is open, and a task tracked after close is aborted at once. Within one process, only one pool registers the identity mailbox on a relay host. Default off, so transient pools never displace the serving registration.
- **Surfaces:** `tendril serve` opts in with its relay auth, re-syncs its group pools at startup, on its 250 ms tick, and after each ingested `0x0A` or `0x0B`, so a relay-only member opens its pools and registers without an inbound stream, and leaves the `/v1/relay` URL it serves with `--relay` to its own listener; `group sync` and `group broadcast` pass relay auth and never register. Python `group_open(..., relay_listen=True)` and Node `groupOpen(groupId, listenAddr, relayListen)` opt in and pass the endpoint's relay auth, and leave the relay hosts of URLs served with `serve_relay` on the same endpoint before the open to its own listener. The FFI group hub is unchanged for now: it neither registers nor passes relay auth for `/v2/relay`.
- **Relay (`crates/tendril-relay/src/server.rs`):** a pending `/v1/relay` or `/v2/relay` dial now fails with `offline` as soon as the control connection that got its `incoming` ends, instead of holding the dialer until the 15 s `accept_timeout`. A pool that rotates or drops a group mailbox aborts its listener, and a dial that reached the relay just before the relay saw that socket close used to hang, so a client gave up after its 10 s control read timeout rather than reading `offline`. An `accept` that already claimed the dial wins: the dialer withdraws its pending entry under the relay table lock before reporting `offline` or `timeout`, and waits for the acceptor when the claim got there first, so the two sockets never get conflicting replies (`spec/relay-v1.md` Section 2.2, `spec/relay-v2.md` Section 3.2, `docs/design/relay-and-runtime.md`; `relay_fails_a_pending_dial_offline_when_its_registration_ends`, `relay_an_accept_that_claimed_a_dial_wins_over_registration_end`, and their `/v2/relay` twins, ordered through a hidden `RelayConfig::test_hook`).
- **Spec (`spec/groups-v1.md` Section 8.1):** relay mailbox registration, per-member rotation cutoff, the discovery-only trust limit, `/v1/relay` identity mailboxes, and admin pushes over the ordinary mailbox.
- **Docs:** `docs/design/groups.md` (dialers, inbound, and three limitation rows replacing "Relay sibling links"), `docs/reference/ffi-and-bindings.md` (Python and Node, relay limits), `docs/usage/cli-demo.md`.
- **Tests:** `crates/tendril-runtime/tests/group_relay.rs` against an embedded relay (relay-only link, per-member rotation, relay auth, transient pools, admin sync over the ordinary mailbox, `/v1/relay` link and revocation); unit tests for close during bind, close racing the listener spawn, track after close, rotation during a relay accept, release on a failing refresh, duplicate identity mailbox claims, plan rules (expiry, wire floor), and hint parsing; `group_e2e_relay_only_serve_member_registers_its_group_mailbox` in `crates/tendril-cli/tests/group_e2e.rs`; Python and Node relay sibling tests against an auth-required `tendril-relay`.
