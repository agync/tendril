Add `PowerProfile::LowPower` and `PathPolicyConfig::low_power` across the Rust core, runtime, C ABI, CLI, and language bindings while keeping `PowerProfile::Standard` defaults unchanged. In `LowPower` mode, the runtime coalesces Wire v1 and v2 relay keepalives (`55 s..=75 s`), runs `/v2/relay` on one mid-hour socket with boundary sleep timers (`+-120 s` skew overlap, zero standby sockets), stops the `InboundRouter` periodic sweeper (`0 Hz`), replaces the `StateStore` poll loop with a `Notify` watcher paired with `RosterStamp` caching and `open_sealed_existing_fast`, and exposes `RouteAnnouncer::set_external_monitor`. Candidate dialing filters non-loopback IPv4/IPv6 address family mismatches via `LocalReachability` (`allows_ip` keeps loopback), honors last-known-good `preferred` ordering, probes hints concurrently under `race_candidates` deadlines, shares an idle-closing `P2pDialer` (`60 s` teardown), resets opt-in `idle_timeout` timers only on application events, and doubles wake retries exponentially (`250 ms` to `wake_retry_max_delay_ms`, `4000 ms` default, `6` retries by default), including Wire v2 stored-route wake. See [relay and runtime design](../../design/relay-and-runtime.md), [providers and relay ladder](../../design/providers-and-relay-ladder.md), and [wake providers](../../design/wake-providers.md).

Expose the profile and lifecycle controls through the FFI, CLI, Python, Node, Go, Swift, Kotlin/JNI, and `@tendril-sdk/web` surfaces, and replace fixed mobile background timers with event-driven OS callbacks. Swift adds `TendrilPowerProfile`, defaults `.extensionClientOnly` and Apple background coordinators to `.lowPower`, avoids blocking expiring-activity guards in the main app process, wires `NWPathMonitor` into `setExternalPathMonitor`, and lengthens `TendrilLocalPushSession` polling to `300000 ms`. Android drops `TendrilForegroundService`'s 60 s timer and deduplicates canonical `NetworkSnapshots` updates into `setExternalPathMonitor`, while `@tendril-sdk/web` adds `lowPower`, `#lkgRoutes`, and app-data-only `idleTimeoutMs`. See [Apple platform](../../design/apple-platform.md), [FFI and bindings](../../reference/ffi-and-bindings.md), and [Web and mobile SDKs](../../reference/web-and-mobile.md).
