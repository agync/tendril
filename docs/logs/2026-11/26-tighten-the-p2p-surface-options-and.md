Tighten the P2P surface options and enrolment routes after review. Group dial commands validate --p2p-bind even when P2P is off, the Node enrol driver runs on a blocking thread, enrol persists in-memory CRLs before the store-backed driver reads the roster, and the docs state that the default all-signed enrol fails against hosts that predate route sets (enrol with committed-only or none).
